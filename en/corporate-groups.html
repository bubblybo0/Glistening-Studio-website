Teams & corporate

Creative workshop for teams and corporate groups in South Holland

Making something together gives a conversation a different start.

Choosing a crystal, combining colours, glancing over at your neighbour's design. During a suncatcher workshop you get creative together, and space opens up to get to know each other in a different way.

At Glistening Studio, each participant makes their own crystal light-catcher, whether you're a team, entrepreneurs or a networking group that barely knows one another yet. A shared activity with room for personal conversations and new ideas.

Afterwards, everyone takes a suncatcher home as a keepsake. And then you enjoy it long after: as soon as the sun hits the crystals, tiny rainbows dance through your home.

Room to really meet one another

Set the busy calendars and emails aside for a moment, and be present in the now. Making something together, screen-free, with time for a conversation.

With materials and designs on the table, you have an instant starting point. What draws you to that colour? How did you come up with that idea? It's a way to discover another side of the people around you, whether that's your team, fellow entrepreneurs or new faces in your network who barely know each other yet.

Hands busy, with room for your own ideas

I'm Kiki, the maker behind Glistening Studio: a nature lover, endlessly curious, and happiest working with my hands amid colour and sparkle. I'll guide you through making a suncatcher: a window crystal that scatters sunlight into tiny rainbows across the room.

Crystals, beads, charms and example designs are ready and waiting. You choose, combine and assemble the pieces yourself: follow an example or make your own design. No experience needed; everyone works at their own pace and I help along the way.

A personal reminder of your time together

Each participant takes a handmade suncatcher home, a design of their own, created during a shared experience.

In a sunny window the crystals come to life: rays of light turn into dancing rainbows on walls and floors. That way, something tangible remains of your time together, long after the workshop.

At your location or in a characterful studio

I come to you on location (your office, a rented space or your event venue) or we work in a characterful studio, in the region around The Hague. Would you rather meet in another city? Then we'll find a characterful studio there together.

Frequently asked questions

What do we do during the connecting workshop?

Each participant makes their own crystal suncatcher from crystals, beads and a metal charm, and takes home their own beautiful handmade keepsake. And then you enjoy it long after: as soon as the sun hits the crystals, tiny rainbows dance through your home.

How long does the workshop take?

About 2 to 2.5 hours. This can be shorter or longer, by arrangement. Let me know how much time is available within your programme when you get in touch.

Is creative experience needed?

No. Examples are ready and waiting, and I guide everyone through the making: follow an example or make your own design.

Where can you meet up?

At your location (office, a rented space or your event venue) or in a characterful studio, around The Hague. Would you rather meet in another city? Then we'll find a characterful studio there together. Materials and guidance are included.

How many participants is the workshop suitable for?

Let me know the expected number of participants when you get in touch, and we'll discuss what's possible for your group and location.

What does a group workshop cost?

Group bookings are discussed on a tailored basis. Mention your group size, preferred place and any wishes, so we can work out the price and any location and travel costs.
