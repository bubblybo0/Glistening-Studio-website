Teams & corporate

Creative workshop for teams and corporate groups in South Holland

Making something together gives a conversation a different start.

Choosing a crystal, combining colours, glancing over at your neighbour's design. During a suncatcher workshop you get creative with your hands, and space opens up to get to know each other in a different way.

At Glistening Studio, colleagues, entrepreneurs or business contacts each make their own crystal light-catcher. A shared activity with room for personal conversations, curiosity and new ideas.

Afterwards, everyone takes a personal keepsake home. As soon as direct sunlight hits the crystals, the suncatcher sends tiny rainbows dancing through the room.

I run corporate workshops on location throughout South Holland. I also work with several studios in The Hague.

Time for the people behind the work

On a working day, attention quickly turns to schedules, projects and everything that still needs doing. During this workshop there's time to try something new together and to meet each other beyond the daily routine.

With materials and designs on the table, you have an instant starting point for a conversation. What draws you to that colour? How did you come up with that idea? And what do you actually enjoy making outside of work?

For colleagues, that creates room to discover another side of each other. During a networking event, it gives participants a shared activity to get acquainted around, even when they barely know one another yet.

Hands busy, with room for your own ideas

I'm Kiki, the maker behind Glistening Studio. During the workshop I guide you through making a suncatcher: a window crystal that scatters direct sunlight into tiny rainbows across the room.

Crystals, beads, metal charms and example designs are ready and waiting. You choose materials, try out combinations and assemble the pieces yourself. That way a personal light-catcher takes shape, step by step.

Participants can follow an example or make their own design. No creative experience is needed. Everyone works at their own pace and I help along the way.

While you're making, there's room to exchange ideas, glance over at each other's work and strike up a conversation.

A personal reminder of your time together

Each participant takes a handmade suncatcher home. A design of their own, created during a shared experience.

In a sunny window the crystals come to life: rays of light turn into dancing rainbows on walls, plants and floors. That way, something tangible remains of the time you spent together, long after the workshop.

Frequently asked questions

What do we do during the corporate workshop?

Each participant makes their own crystal suncatcher with crystals, beads and a metal charm. The workshop combines creative work with your hands with room for connecting with one another.

How long does the workshop take?

The workshop takes about 2 to 2.5 hours. Let me know how much time is available within your programme when you get in touch.

Is creative experience needed?

No. Examples are ready and waiting, and I guide participants through the making. Everyone can follow an example or put together their own design.

What's included?

All the materials you need and the guidance are included. Each participant takes their own suncatcher home afterwards. Any wishes for snacks and drinks we discuss when you get in touch.

Can you come to our office or an event venue?

Yes, I run workshops on location throughout South Holland. Send the place and a short description of the space, and we'll discuss the practical options.

Can you also offer a location in The Hague?

I work with several studios in The Hague. We'll look at the options based on your group size, wishes and availability.

How many participants is the workshop suitable for?

Let me know the expected number of participants when you get in touch. Then we'll discuss what's possible for your group and location.

What does a corporate group workshop cost?

Corporate group bookings are discussed on a tailored basis. Mention your group size, preferred location and any additional wishes, so we can work out the price and any location and travel costs.
