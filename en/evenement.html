An afternoon full of light

You'll get to work with faceted crystals, beads, and a metal charm of your choice — a tree of life, butterfly, mandala, or hummingbird. Step by step you build your own suncatcher, with all the room to play, hesitate, and choose.

No experience needed. All materials are ready and waiting — you choose the combination that suits you.

  • New knowledge about crystals and how they catch the light
  • A screen-free afternoon, hands busy and mind at ease
