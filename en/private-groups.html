For a girls' day out, a birthday or just spending time together

That birthday you want to celebrate in a special way. The get-together with friends that's been sitting in the group chat for ages. A creative afternoon during a hen party. Or simply the urge to do something fun together.

A suncatcher workshop is also a great fit for a sisters' day, a family day out, a reunion, or an outing with your book club, walking group or society.

Maybe you've known each other for years. Maybe different circles of friends are meeting for the first time. As you pick out materials and create your designs, there's plenty on the table to talk about: a colour that suits someone perfectly, a special charm, or a combination you'd never have thought of yourself.

Making with your hands, your own way

I'm Kiki, the maker behind Glistening Studio. I guide you through making a personal suncatcher with crystals, beads and metal charms.

You choose, combine and assemble the pieces yourself. That way you watch loose materials turn, step by step, into a design that's completely your own.

There are examples ready to help you on your way. You can follow an example or try out your own ideas. No experience needed: you work at your own pace and I'm there to help along the way.

With something creative to focus on, there's room to lose yourself in the making, to ask each other for ideas, and to catch up along the way.

A keepsake that keeps sparkling at home

Afterwards you take your handmade suncatcher home with you. Hang it in a window where direct sunlight comes in. As soon as the light shines on the crystals, dancing rainbows appear on the wall, among your plants or across the floor.

That way your shared moment gets a place in your home. A personal keepsake that can carry you straight back to that afternoon or evening together.

Frequently asked questions

What do we make during the workshop?

Everyone makes their own crystal suncatcher. You work with crystals, beads and a metal charm, and take your creation home with you afterwards.

How long does the suncatcher workshop take?

The workshop takes about 2 to 2.5 hours.

Do we need any creative experience?

No, no experience is needed. Example designs are ready and waiting, and I guide you through the making. You can follow an example or put together your own design.

Do we need to bring any materials?

All the materials you need are included. For a workshop at your own location, we discuss the set-up and what needs to be there beforehand.

Where can we take the workshop?

I run group workshops on location throughout South Holland. I also work with several studios in The Hague. We choose the location together, to suit your group and availability.

What does a group workshop cost?

We put together a tailored booking for your group. Include the expected number of participants and your preferred location with your request. Then we discuss the price, any location and travel costs, and the options for snacks and drinks.

How many people can take part?

Let me know how many participants you expect when you get in touch. I'll tell you what's possible for your group and your chosen location.

Turn "we should plan something" into a date

Email info@glisteningstudio.com with your preferred date or period, the number of people, and where you'd like to take the workshop. Is there something to celebrate? Feel free to mention it.

Then we'll look together at how your creative moment could take shape.
